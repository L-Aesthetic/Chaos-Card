Privacy Policy

Effective date: December 25, 2025
Site: https://doomgo.world
Contact: doomgo2026@gmail.com

Doomgo is a prediction/bingo game. This Privacy Policy explains what data Doomgo collects, how it’s used, and the choices you have.

1) What Doomgo collects

A) Data stored on your device

  • Your boards, hits, settings, profile name, and app state are stored locally on your device using localStorage.

B) Anonymous device identifier

  • Doomgo creates an anonymous device ID (stored in your browser) so the app can function consistently and so we can see basic usage in the admin dashboard.

C) Basic “presence” / admin visibility

  • To understand active usage and keep Doomgo stable, Doomgo may send a small profile record to our database provider (Supabase), including: anonymous device ID, last-seen timestamp, a rough region guess (from browser locale), and your browser user-agent string.

D) Board logging (game analytics)

  • Doomgo may log board events (for example, when a board is locked or a new deck is activated) including: board type, whether it’s locked, a “chaos score,” and the tile text you’re playing with.
  • This is used to improve deck quality, balance, and reliability (for example, preventing broken center tiles or duplicate tokens).

E) AI features (Oracle / decks / captions)

  • When you use AI features (deck generation, Oracle text, roasts, captions, headlines), Doomgo sends the text you provide (and/or the tile text) to an AI model provider (Gemini) through a server function (Netlify) to generate a response.
  • We do not ask for or require your real name, phone number, or address to use Doomgo’s AI features.

F) Ads (Google AdSense)

  • Doomgo may show ads using Google AdSense. Ad providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure, and personalize ads (where permitted).
  • In the EEA/UK/Switzerland, a consent message may be shown so you can choose how ads are delivered.

G) Payments (Stripe)

  • If you purchase Doomgo Plus, payments are processed by Stripe. Doomgo does not store your full card number. Stripe may collect payment and billing information per their policies.

H) Third-party avatar loading (Dicebear)

  • If you don’t upload a profile image, Doomgo may load an avatar from Dicebear based on a seed. Loading that avatar may make a request to Dicebear’s servers.

2) What Doomgo does not collect

  • No required accounts or passwords for basic play.
  • No selling of your personal information.
  • No “secret” third-party analytics SDK required to play the game (ads and payments are separate services).

3) How Doomgo uses data

  • To run the app (boards, saving progress, restoring state).
  • To operate the admin system (seeing active usage, stability, spam prevention, limiting abuse).
  • To improve the experience (deck quality, bug fixes, performance).
  • To provide AI features when you request them.
  • To show ads (if you’re not a Plus user) and measure ad performance.
  • To process purchases (Plus subscriptions) through Stripe.

4) Legal bases (EEA/UK)

  • Consent for certain ad-related cookies/processing where required (via consent message).
  • Legitimate interests for basic security, preventing abuse, and understanding app stability.
  • Contract for providing paid features if you subscribe to Doomgo Plus.

5) Sharing & service providers

Doomgo uses third parties to operate the service, including:

  • Supabase (database/auth/data storage for presence and board logs)
  • Netlify (hosting and server functions)
  • Google (AdSense and Gemini)
  • Stripe (payments)
  • Dicebear (avatar images, if used)

These providers may process data on our behalf. Their use of data is governed by their own policies and agreements.

6) Data retention

  • LocalStorage data remains on your device until you clear it, reset the app, or clear site data in your browser.
  • Presence/board logs may be retained as long as needed for operations, security, and product improvement. We may delete, aggregate, or anonymize data over time.

7) Your choices

  • You can clear Doomgo data anytime using the in-app “Reset App Data” button.
  • You can clear site storage in your browser settings (cookies/site data/local storage).
  • In EEA/UK/Switzerland, you can manage ad consent via the consent message when shown.
  • If you want us to delete server-side records tied to your anonymous device ID, email us at doomgo2026@gmail.com with your request. (If needed, we may ask for the device ID stored by Doomgo to locate your record.)

8) Children

Doomgo is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child provided personal data to Doomgo, contact us and we will address it.

9) International transfers

Doomgo and its providers may process data in countries other than where you live. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

10) Updates

We may update this policy as Doomgo evolves. The effective date at the top will change when we do.
